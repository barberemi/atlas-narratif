# Atlas Narratif

> Online tool for narrative structuring and analysis, designed for novel writers.

Atlas Narratif helps authors organize, visualize, and verify the consistency of their manuscripts. Data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database with encryption at rest.

## Public pages

- [Home](https://atlas-narratif.com/) : create or import a project
- [Blog](https://atlas-narratif.com/blog) : tips and guides on writing and narrative structure
- [Sign in](https://atlas-narratif.com/login) : log in to an existing account

- [Privacy](https://atlas-narratif.com/privacy) : privacy policy (GDPR)
- [Terms](https://atlas-narratif.com/terms) : terms and conditions

## Key features

- Narrative timeline: organize events by chapter, drag-and-drop, filter by volume
- Lore management: character, location, and object sheets with cross-references
- Save the Cat: 15-beat structure with per-chapter progress tracking
- Hero's Journey: Joseph Campbell's 12 stages applied to your manuscript
- Emotional arc: intensity curve per chapter with graphical visualization
- Inconsistency detection: automatic spotting of contradictions (dates, locations, attributes)
- Relationship graph: interactive visualization of links between entities
- Interactive map: character journeys on a map with waypoints
- Plants and payoffs: tracking of narrative setups and their resolution
- Narrative threads: subplot management and progression tracking
- Multi-volume support: global filtering by volume, cross-volume plants
- AI import: manuscript analysis via prompts compatible with ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude
